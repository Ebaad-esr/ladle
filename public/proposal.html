PROJECT DEEP DIVE

1. Genesis: Problem & Solution

The Problem: The steel industry's reliance on manual, visual tracking of critical assets like ladles is an archaic process that introduces significant operational friction, data inaccuracy, and reactive maintenance cycles, ultimately impacting throughput and profitability.
Our Solution: We propose an end-to-end Industrial IoT (IIoT) system that automates the entire tracking process, providing real-time visibility and enriching the collected data for proactive operational management.
